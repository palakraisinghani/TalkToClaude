Lead your Claude team by voice

Talk to Ada, your assistant. She sets up projects, hands out the work and tells you who needs you, while each Claude Code agent works away in its own folder on your computer.

Download How it works

A team, not a chat

Everything you'd do in Claude Code, on a call, with your hands free.

One agent per projectAda sets up a folder and a Claude Code agent for each project. One has the floor; the rest keep working in the background.
Talk over anyoneCut in whenever you like. Nothing they started is lost, and questions from the team wait until you're free.
Plan mode and more"Plan mode", "think harder", "new conversation", undo, models and effort: by voice, a button or a /command.
Asks before anything riskyAgents pause for your yes, and anything that can't be undone needs a "confirm". Their changes can be rolled back.
On your own computerYour projects and voice stay with you. Speech is recognised on your machine and learns how you say your team's names.
From your phone tooJoin the call from your phone or tablet and approve your team's requests from anywhere in the house.

You'll need Claude Code, signed in.

Every agent is a Claude Code session, so it runs on your Claude subscription (Pro or Max) or Console account. No API key needed. Not installed yet? ClaudeCall walks you through it when you open it, or in a terminal:

curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash
claude auth login

Up and running in a minute

Three steps, then just talk.

InstallMac: open the .dmg and drag ClaudeCall to Applications. Ubuntu/Debian: sudo apt install ./ClaudeCall-linux-x64.deb
Open ClaudeCallIt opens in its own window and runs in the background, so your team keeps working when you close it.
Press call and talk"Ada, start a project for a recipe website. Call the agent Kiwi." Say a name to talk to that agent.
